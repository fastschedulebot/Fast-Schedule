Why Verification Is Required To unlock the Referral Program you must connect a channel and schedule at least 1 message . This is not bureaucracy — it is how we verify your account is real. Why we do it. Every referral reward is paid out as Premium time, and Premium time costs us real money (Telegram Stars fees and server costs). Without verification, anyone could spin up dozens of empty accounts in minutes and drain the reward pool — which would force us to shut the program down for everyone, including honest users. What counts as verification. Connecting a channel proves you have something real to post to; scheduling a message proves you actually use the bot. Both checks are automatic and take most users under two minutes: send your channel link in Step 1 of the tutorial, then schedule any post — even a test message to yourself counts. What we check — and what we don't. We only look at anonymous counters (channels connected, messages created). We never read your message contents, never ask for documents or phone numbers, and never share anything with third parties. Verification is purely about activity, not identity. Common confusion. Opening the Referral menu before verifying shows a locked screen — that is expected, not a bug. Finish the two steps above and the menu unlocks by itself; nothing else is required. See also: FAQ
