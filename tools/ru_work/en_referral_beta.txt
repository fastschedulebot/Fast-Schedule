Beta Program The Referral Program is currently in BETA . It works end to end — links, tracking, crediting and day activation are all live — but you may occasionally run into rough edges: a counter that updates with a delay, a list that needs a reopen to refresh, or a message that reads slightly differently from what you expected. Your earned days are safe. Beta status affects polish, never balances. Every qualifying referral is recorded the moment it happens, and Premium days, once earned, are never lost because of a display glitch. If something looks wrong, reopen the /referral menu first — most counters refresh on open. Help us finish it. If you spot an error, tell us through /feedback with what you did step by step and, if possible, a screenshot. Beta reporters directly shape the final version: unclear texts get rewritten and confusing flows get simplified because of your reports. What may change. Reward sizes, limits and rules can still be tuned while the program is in beta. Any change will be announced in the referral menu itself — earned balances are always honored under the rules that were live when they were earned. See also: FAQ
