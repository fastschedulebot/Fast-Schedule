How Crediting Works Here is exactly what happens from click to Premium days, for both sides. 1. Click. A new user opens your link and starts the bot. The click is recorded instantly, but nothing is granted yet — this is what the "pending" state in your list means. 2. Verify. The new user connects a channel and schedules at least 1 message — the same verification you completed to unlock the program. Until then, the referral stays pending. 3. Credit. The moment they qualify, both of you receive 3 Premium days : the days land in your referral balance (see them with /referral under Available days). If you already have paid Premium, the days wait in your balance until it ends. 4. Activate. On the free plan, activate earned days from the referral menu — a Premium period starts immediately. Used days move to the Used counter so you always see earned, used and available totals. Limits. Each user can generate referral rewards from up to 15 referees, i.e. a 45-day total. One referral per user — the same newcomer can never credit you twice, even if they click your link again. See also: FAQ
