Only anonymous polls may post in channels — that is a Telegram rule for channels, not a bot limit.\n\n<b>Special content types</b>\n• <b>Location and venues</b> — send the location during scheduling.\n• <b>Voice messages and video notes</b> — recorded or forwarded as usual.\n• <b>Animated GIFs and stickers</b> — stickers can even carry a text caption, posted right after the sticker.\n\n<b>Time zones</b>\nEvery date you enter is interpreted in the <b>channel's time zone</b>, so a channel with an international audience posts exactly when its readers are awake. Set or check it under <b>Connected Channel</b> → channel → <b>Time Zone</b>, or with <code>/timezone</code>."
