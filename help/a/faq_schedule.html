Scheduling FAQ

Questions people most often ask about scheduling: formats, editing, limits, multiple channels, media, recurring messages, and more. Tap a question below to see the answer.

Common questions

Do I need a sender bot to schedule messages?

Yes. After connecting a channel, connect a sender bot for it from Bots - scheduling unlocks once every channel has its own bot. Posts then appear from your bot's name, and you also get lower delivery delays and per-channel statistics.

Can I edit a message after I schedule it?

Yes. Open Message List (or use /list), tap the message, then Edit — you can change the text, the date/time, the media, or even the target channel. Use /edit with the job ID for a direct shortcut.

How many scheduled messages can I store?

Free plan: 100 pending messages stored at once. Premium: unlimited. If you go over the free limit you will receive a notice listing the excess; messages stay frozen but are not deleted.

How many messages can I send per month?

Free plan: 100 sent messages per channel per calendar month. Premium: unlimited sends. There is no daily cap on either plan.

Can I schedule the same message to multiple channels?

Each scheduled message targets one channel. Free plan: 1 channel. Premium: up to 3 channels, each with its own sender bot, time zone, and signature.

Can I schedule a poll or quiz?

Yes. Create the poll/quiz in any chat and forward it to the bot during scheduling — or build it right in the scheduling flow, where supported. Note: Telegram only allows anonymous polls in channels.

What media can I attach to a scheduled message?

Photos, videos, GIFs, documents, and albums. Free plan videos: up to 25MB (no 4K). Premium: up to 50MB with 4K support. Use Normal, Random, or Sequential media modes.

Can I schedule a recurring message (daily / weekly / monthly)?

Yes. Free plan: 1 recurring message. Premium: unlimited. Set the interval and time, then manage it (edit, pause, delete) from Message List.

Can I use HH:MM to schedule for today?

Yes. When entering the date, a time in HH:MM format schedules the message for today at that time. Full dates like 2026-08-20 14:30 also work.

Can I upload a .txt file to schedule many messages at once?

Yes. Send a .txt file and the bot parses it into multiple scheduled posts. This is the fastest way to build a month of content — combine it with the SetDate companion bot.

What time zone is used for my scheduled times?

Each channel has its own time zone (set in Channel Settings), which defaults to your account time zone. Verify the listed time zone before confirming a schedule.

What happens if my Premium expires during a schedule?

Nothing is deleted right away. Messages to non-kept channels stop sending after a grace period, and you get a prompt to download a full .fsback backup you can restore later with /import.

How do I see everything I have scheduled?

Open Message List (or /list). You can preview, edit, pause, or delete any scheduled or recurring message there.

Can I schedule a forwarded message?

Yes. During scheduling, forward a message from any chat — the bot copies its text and media into the scheduled post. You can edit the caption before confirming.

Can I schedule an album of photos?

Yes. Send several photos together during the media step and choose Normal mode — they post as a single grouped album.
