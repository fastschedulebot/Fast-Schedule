Recurring Tips

  • Use recurring messages for daily digests, weekly roundups, or monthly posts.
  • Premium unlocks unlimited recurring messages.
  • Recurring messages count separately from one-time scheduled messages.
  • On Premium expiry you keep up to 1 recurring message — choose which.

See Recurring for setup.

Common questions

Can recurring messages be paused?

Yes — open the recurring message in Message List to pause, edit, or delete it. Paused messages stop sending until resumed.
