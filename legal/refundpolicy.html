1. Overview

This Refund Policy explains how refunds work for Premium subscriptions to Fast Scheduler ("the Service"), operated by Maxim Mkrtchyan, an individual operator based in Armenia ("we", "us"). Premium is paid exclusively with Telegram Stars (in-app virtual currency), processed entirely by Telegram.

Because Telegram Stars purchases are governed by Telegram's own policies, refunds are limited. Please read this policy before purchasing.

2. How Stars Billing Works

  • Monthly plans renew automatically every 30 days until cancelled. Cancel anytime via the in-bot /premium menu (cancellation is additionally registered at Telegram); cancellation stops future charges but does not refund the current period.
  • 3-month, 6-month, and yearly plans are one-time prepaid purchases and do not auto-renew.
  • Cooling-off / right of withdrawal (EU/UK): For digitally supplied services, the 14-day right of withdrawal generally does not apply once delivery/performance has begun with your prior explicit consent (i.e., once your Premium features are activated). Where the right does apply (e.g., you cancel before any Premium feature is used), you may be entitled to a remedy under applicable law.

3. Discretionary Refunds

We do not refund Telegram Stars purchases as a rule — Stars transactions are final under Telegram's policies. Outside mandatory consumer rights, we may grant a refund at our sole discretion where, for example:
- You were charged twice for the same subscription period;
- A technical fault on our side prevented you from using the Service you paid for; or
- We terminated your access without cause.

Discretionary refunds are evaluated case by case, and only where Telegram's platform permits a reversal. Granting a refund in one case does not create a precedent.

How an approved refund is processed

  • Your Premium access is revoked immediately, the refund is recorded with a timestamp, and you receive an in-bot notification.
  • The funds return exclusively through Telegram within its processing constraints. We cannot transfer money to you directly.
  • Refunds, where granted, cover the current paid period in full; partial (pro-rated) refunds are not offered.
  • Referral Premium days and promo-code free periods have no monetary value and are never refundable; where Premium was granted partly through referrals, only the paid portion counts.

4. How to Request a Refund

  1. Email fastschedulebot@gmail.com with your transaction ID, Telegram username, and reason.
  2. Include the date of purchase and the email/account used where applicable.

We aim to acknowledge refund requests within 7 business days.

5. Auto-Renewal and Cancellation

  • Stars monthly plans renew automatically until cancelled. Cancel via in-bot /premium to stop future billing. Already-paid periods are not refunded solely for cancellation.
  • Stars 3-month, 6-month, and yearly plans are one-time purchases and do not auto-renew.
  • Downgrading from Premium to Free takes effect at the end of the paid period.

6. Trials and Promo Codes

  • Any free period or welcome offer is provided at no cost; converting to a paid plan results in a charge per the prices in our Terms.
  • Promo codes may reduce or waive a charge; they do not create an independent refund right.
  • Where a promo resulted in no payment, no refund is applicable.

7. Chargebacks and Abuse

  • Abuse of chargebacks (e.g., initiating a bank chargeback after receiving the Service) may result in termination of access and, where permitted, pursuit of the amount owed.
  • No refund is granted for accounts terminated for Terms of Service violations (see Terms, Acceptable Use).

8. Processing Times

  • Approved refunds are processed within 7 business days where technically feasible, and appear according to Telegram's own timelines.
