## 2. How Stars Billing Works

- **Monthly plans** renew automatically every 30 days until cancelled. Cancel anytime via the in-bot **`/premium`** menu (cancellation is additionally registered at Telegram); cancellation stops future charges but does not refund the current period.
- **3-month and 6-month prepaid plans** are one-time purchases at a discounted rate (currently 10% and 13% off the plain monthly price respectively) and do **not** auto-renew.
- **Yearly plans** are one-time prepaid purchases at a discounted rate (currently 17% off twelve months at the monthly price) and do **not** auto-renew.
- Reference USD prices are listed in our Terms of Service; the **in-bot prices at checkout are authoritative** (plans are billed in Telegram Stars at Telegram's rate).
- **Cooling-off / right of withdrawal (EU/UK):** For digitally supplied services, the 14-day right of withdrawal generally **does not apply once delivery/performance has begun** with your prior explicit consent (i.e., once your Premium features are activated). Where the right does apply (e.g., you cancel before any Premium feature is used), you may be entitled to a remedy under applicable law.

## 3. Discretionary Refunds

We **do not** refund Telegram Stars purchases as a rule — Stars transactions are final under Telegram's policies. Outside mandatory consumer rights, we may grant a refund at our sole discretion where, for example:
- You were charged twice for the same subscription period;
- A technical fault on our side prevented you from using the Service you paid for; or
- We terminated your access without cause.

Discretionary refunds are evaluated case by case, and only where Telegram's platform permits a reversal. Granting a refund in one case does not create a precedent.

### How an approved refund is processed
- Your Premium access is revoked immediately, the refund is recorded with a timestamp, and you receive an in-bot notification.
- The funds return exclusively through Telegram within its processing constraints. We cannot transfer money to you directly.
- Refunds, where granted, cover the current paid period in full; partial (pro-rated) refunds are not offered.
- Referral Premium days and promo-code free periods have no monetary value and are never refundable; where Premium was granted partly through referrals, only the paid portion counts.
